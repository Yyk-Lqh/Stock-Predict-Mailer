# Code of Conduct

English · [简体中文](#chinese)

## Our commitment

Stock-Predict-Mailer welcomes people across backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Participants should be able to ask questions, contribute, and disagree without harassment or exposure of private information.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss reproducible behavior and evidence; challenge ideas respectfully.
- Make space for newcomers and people with different experiences.
- Give specific, constructive feedback; acknowledge and correct mistakes.
- Protect private identities, documents, credentials, and research data.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, doxxing, and retaliatory behavior are not acceptable. Do not use technical disagreement to target a person or group.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces, and when officially representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or exclude participants. Responses should be proportionate to the impact, severity, recurrence, and risk; possible steps include a private request to stop, a warning, temporary restrictions, or removal.

Maintainers should handle reports fairly, minimize disclosure of personal information, explain decisions when appropriate, and avoid retaliation. If a maintainer is implicated, request contact with another uninvolved maintainer where one is available. No confidential reporting address is published at present: ask the [maintainer](https://github.com/Yyk-Lqh) for a private contact channel without publicly identifying affected people or sharing the incident. If a public contact request is necessary, keep it free of incident details. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), rather than using a public conduct report.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy carries forward principles adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It is not a claim that every part of that template is reproduced verbatim.

## 简体中文

### 承诺与期望

欢迎不同背景、身份、能力和经验的参与者，保障提问、贡献和讨论不受骚扰或隐私曝光。围绕可复现行为与证据讨论，尊重不同经历，给新人参与空间；反馈具体且有建设性，承认并修正错误，保护私人身份、文档、凭据及研究资料。

不接受骚扰、威胁、性化关注、歧视、侮辱、人身攻击、曝光隐私或报复，不借技术争议针对个人或群体。

### 范围、报告与执行

适用于仓库 Issue、审查、讨论等项目空间及正式代表项目的场合。维护者可删除有害内容、限制互动或取消参与资格，并根据影响、严重性、重复性与风险采取私下劝止、警告、临时限制或移除等相称措施。

报告应公平处理、尽量减少个人信息披露、适当解释决定且不得报复。涉及某位维护者时，尽可能请求未涉事维护者处理。当前没有公布专门私密举报地址；向[维护者](https://github.com/Yyk-Lqh)请求私密联系渠道时，不公开受影响人员或事件内容。如必须公开请求联系，仅说明需要私密渠道，不附事件细节。漏洞另按[安全策略](SECURITY.md#chinese)处理。

本项目规则延续原行为准则中改编自 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 的原则，并非该模板的逐字完整副本。
